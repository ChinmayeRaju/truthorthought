Experiment Instructions

Please read these instructions carefully before proceeding.

What you will do:
  • You will analyze a news article about politics, war, or current affairs
  • The article will be displayed on the left side of your screen
  • Our AI system will provide fact vs. opinion analysis on the right side
  • Take your time to read both the original article and the analysis
  • Your interactions with the interface will be logged for research purposes
What we're studying:
  • How AI-assisted fact-checking affects bias perception
  • The effectiveness of distinguishing facts from opinions
  • User interaction patterns with bias analysis tools
  • The impact on media literacy and critical thinking
Privacy and Data:
  • All data collected is anonymous and for research purposes only
  • No personal information will be shared or published
  • You can withdraw from the study at any time
  • Results will be used for academic research and improving AI systems
